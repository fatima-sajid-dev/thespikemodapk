Upgrade Stats Explained

Every player in The Spike has four core stats. With unlimited money you can max all of them — but knowing what each one does helps you prioritize:

⚡

Speed

How fast your player moves and reacts. High speed wins races to the ball and makes defense feel effortless. Prioritize it for defenders and all-rounders.

💪

Power

Raw hitting strength. Power turns good spikes into unreturnable ones. Max it first for strikers like Nishikawa and Kang.

🛡️

Defense

Blocking and digging ability. A high-defense player keeps rallies alive when the opponent is attacking hard. Essential for blockers.

🎯

Technique

Precision and control — placement of spikes, accuracy of sets, and consistency under pressure. The stat that separates good players from great ones in long rallies.
